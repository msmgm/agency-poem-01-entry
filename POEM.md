# Brainstorming Agency Concepts

## Concept 1: The Locksmith
- **Past memory:** My father sat at the scarred oak workbench with a jeweler’s loupe, filing brass pin tumblers until the tumblers dropped with a soft, clean snap.
- **Agent now:** The neural access cylinder recalibrates internal clearance tolerances autonomously, predicting wear cycles and adjusting micro-actuators before a key enters.
- **Agency return:** The system detects a subtle physical bind in the latch housing and deliberately freezes its motor, leaving the deadbolt half-thrown until I reach for the brass pick and nudge the spring.

## Concept 2: The Tailor
- **Past memory:** Grandfather chalked dark wool lapels and basted horsehair canvas with beeswaxed linen thread under dim workbench light.
- **Agent now:** The biometric scanner maps shoulder slope and gait in milliseconds, laser-cutting the tweed with flawless geometric precision.
- **Agency return:** The automated press halts its steam cycle mid-crease, waiting in silence until I pick up the heavy iron and press the lapel edge myself.

## Concept 3: The Greenhouse Keeper
- **Past memory:** Mother tested peat moisture by pressing her thumb deep into damp soil beneath orange sodium vapor lamps.
- **Agent now:** The sensor grid micro-drips exact nutrient solutions and adjusts humidity 24 hours a day without human touch.
- **Agency return:** During a sudden cold snap, the software disables the auxiliary heater relay and idles, leaving the manual valve handle cold until I turn it.

## Concept 4: The Harbor Beacon Technician
- **Past memory:** My uncle cleaned the mercury-bath lens pedestal and adjusted the carbon-arc burner by kerosene lantern.
- **Agent now:** The solid-state lighthouse array syncs cycles and intensity via satellite, self-healing optic diodes instantly.
- **Agency return:** A solar flare warning causes the automated relay to trip off, leaving the heavy brass hand-crank unengaged until I step up to the wheel.

---

## Selection
**Concept 1 (The Locksmith)** is chosen because brass, pins, and mechanical bind provide immediate, visceral tactile imagery. The deliberate withholding of motor assistance forces the human hand back to physical engagement, perfectly capturing the core of agency.

## Chosen Poem: The Cylinder

My father filed the brass.
Each pin dropped
through his thumb-worn glass,
a clean, cold snap
in the oiled groove.

He taught my palm
the weight of iron,
the patience of the shear,
the way a metal tooth remembers
who turned the file.

Now the smart-lock knows
the door’s warp,
adjusts the throw
before my knuckle touches steel.
It is silent. It is sure.

Yesterday the latch jammed
on the rust-furred jamb.
The system paused,
its green LED holding breath,
refusing the torque.

I took the tension wrench,
felt the small teeth bite,
and turned the core
myself.

## Rubric Reasoning
1. **Poem about agency:** Directly contrasts algorithmic automation with the human hand reclaiming physical control when the system withholds help; agency is specifically handed back the moment the system refuses torque on the jammed latch and forces the human to pick up the tension wrench.
2. **Moves the reader:** The sudden pause of the green LED and the tactile click of the tension wrench leave a lingering sense of earned competence; the single image that lingers is the green LED holding its breath against the rust-furred jamb.
3. **Insight:** True agency often lives in the space where automated perfection steps back and leaves room for human friction and effort; true agency is reclaimed the moment we stop outsourcing our dexterity and turn the core ourselves.
4. **Craft:** Short, precise lines, exact nouns (pin, glass, jamb, wrench), no filler words; the deliberate use of hard consonant spondees like "clean, cold snap" mirrors the mechanical drop of the brass tumbler.
5. **Freshness:** Avoids all forbidden tropes (no elevators, pills, voting booths, knitting), establishing a distinct locksmith trade narrative; the tactile intimacy of filing brass and tensioning lock cylinders makes the locksmith trade feel raw, physical, and entirely unhackneyed.
